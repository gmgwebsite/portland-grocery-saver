How it works

Portland Grocery Saver is a free weekly guide to the best grocery prices in Portland, Oregon.

Every week we

  1. Check the weekly ads for Fred Meyer, Safeway and New Seasons, which start each Wednesday.
  2. Photograph in-store flyers and coupon books for stores that don't publish full ads online, like Grocery Outlet.
  3. Compare the same items across stores by price per pound or per unit, so a bigger package doesn't fool you.
  4. Publish a roundup of the best deals, a store-by-store guide, cheap meal ideas and money-saving tips.

About WinCo

WinCo doesn't run weekly sales; it keeps prices low every day. We use it as the "everyday price" to beat. A sale elsewhere is only worth a special trip if it beats WinCo.

Accuracy

Prices come from each store's published weekly ad and can vary by location. Some prices need a loyalty card, digital coupon, or minimum purchase. Always check the store's ad or shelf tag before you buy. If you spot a mistake, let us know.

We are not affiliated with any grocery store.
