Store-by-store notes

Safeway (through Tue Nov 10). The 59¢/lb Signature SELECT frozen turkey is the headline. A frozen turkey keeps for months, so there's no reason to wait, and the best sizes can sell out. Also worth grabbing: pork loin chops at $1.99/lb, 16–20 count shrimp at $5.99/lb, King Arthur flour (5 lb) for $5, and sugar (4 lb) for $3.99. Cereal from General Mills is just $1.49 a box.

Fred Meyer (through Tue Nov 10). The 32 oz bag of Kroger cheese for $4.99 is about $2.50 a pound, nearly half of Safeway's sale price. Fresh Atlantic salmon at $10.99/lb is the lowest salmon price we found, and Dungeness crab is $9.99/lb. If you don't bake, a $5 pumpkin or apple pie is less than half the price of Safeway's.

New Seasons (through Tue Nov 10). Not the cheapest store, but this week it's the place for baking extras: selected Simply Organic spices are 50% off, Guittard baking chocolate is 33% off, and Bob's Red Mill organic flour is $5 off a 5 lb bag. Shopping online? Code NOVEMBER15 takes $15 off a pickup or delivery order of $75 or more.

WinCo (no weekly ad). WinCo doesn't run sales flyers; its prices are low every day. In Stumptown Savings' October 2026 survey, WinCo had the cheapest basket of 20 staples in the Portland area ($52.34, versus an average of $93.52). Buy your everyday basics here, and use the sales above for the big-ticket items.

Grocery Outlet. This week's flyer is only published as images, so it isn't included yet. We'll add it once we've photographed the in-store ad.

Thanksgiving stock-up plan (buy this week)

Things that keep well and are at or near their holiday lows:

  • Frozen turkey (Safeway, 59¢/lb). A 14 lb bird is about $8.26.
  • Flour and sugar (Safeway, $5 and $3.99).
  • Spices for stuffing and pie (New Seasons, 50% off selected Simply Organic). Check your cabinet first: sage, thyme, cinnamon and nutmeg go stale after a year or two.
  • Baking chocolate (New Seasons, 33% off) for holiday cookies.
  • Shredded cheese (Fred Meyer, 32 oz for $4.99). It freezes well in the bag.

Wait on fresh produce such as potatoes, celery, onions and cranberries. Those usually hit their lowest prices in the week before Thanksgiving.

Three cheap dinners from this week's ads

  1. Pork chops with roasted apples. Safeway pork chops ($1.99/lb) plus Fred Meyer Honeycrisp apples ($2.49/lb). Feeds four for about $8.
  2. Garlic shrimp pasta. A pound of 16–20 ct shrimp ($5.99 at Safeway), a box of pasta and a lemon. About $9 for four.
  3. Cheesy beef taco night. Ground beef (Safeway, $5/lb in the big pack; freeze the extra), Fred Meyer cheese, and 3-for-$5 avocados.

Money-saving tips for this week

  • Compare the price per pound, not the sticker. Safeway's 24 oz cheese looks cheaper at $6.99 than a $4.99 deal elsewhere until you notice the Fred Meyer bag is 32 oz.
  • "Buy 2 Get 2 Free" only helps if you'd buy four. Safeway's soda and chip deals are good for a party, not for a household that drinks one 12-pack a month.
  • Clip digital coupons before you go. Some sale prices at Safeway and Fred Meyer need the store app or loyalty card. Check the ad terms.
  • Split your trip. If Safeway and Fred Meyer are near each other, a 20-minute second stop for salmon, cheese and pie can save $10–15 on a typical holiday shop.
